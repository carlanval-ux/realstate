# User manual · CLARTO Collaborators

This guide is for you, the agent or agency collaborating with CLARTO. It explains what you see in your collaborator
panel (panel.clarto.es/colaboradores), how notices reach you and how to answer the searches we send you. To find
something quickly, type one or more words in **Search the manual**; on a phone, the **Contents** opens and closes with
its title. And on any screen, the **Ask CLARTO** button at the bottom right opens a chat: write your question as you
would ask someone and it shows you the sections of this manual that explain it. If you type the title, address or
postcode of one of your properties, or the title of a search we sent you, it also shows you those records under **In
your records**, with their link. Only what CLARTO has assigned or sent to you appears, never anything else.

## Signing in to your panel

### Your first password

When CLARTO adds you, you receive an e-mail **"Your access as a CLARTO collaborator"** with the button **Create my
password**. Click it, choose your password and you can sign in. Your user is the e-mail address the invitation was sent
to.

* The link works **only once** and expires after the hours stated in the e-mail (usually 24).
* If it has expired or you cannot find it (check your spam folder too), ask your CLARTO contact to send it again.
* After that, always sign in at panel.clarto.es/colaboradores with your e-mail and password (**Sign in**).

### Forgotten your password?

On the sign-in screen click **Forgot password?**, enter your e-mail and you will receive a link to choose a new one. It
also expires, so use it soon.

### Panel language

The panel, the invitation and the notices reach you in the language CLARTO has on record for you. If you prefer
another one, tell your CLARTO contact.

## What your panel is and what you do not see

Your panel is a **read-only** window: you see what CLARTO shares with you and you answer searches. You cannot create
or change properties here.

* You only see the properties CLARTO has **assigned** to you, and only while the assignment is active.
* You only see the search requests **sent to you** and your own answers.
* For each property you see the authorised data of your collaboration: the area and town, not the exact address. You
  never see data about the owner, buyers or tenants, or other collaborators.
* If you open a link to something that is not yours, the panel answers as if it did not exist.

The left-hand menu has **My properties**, **Requests received** (if CLARTO works with you on requests) and **User
manual**. The search box at the top finds your properties and your requests by their title.

## My properties

### The property list

**My properties** shows each assigned property with these columns:

* **Reference**: CLARTO's reference for the property.
* **Property**: the title, with the area and town below.
* **Relation**: how you collaborate. **Brought by the collaborator** (you brought it), **Co-marketing** (you help us
  sell or let it) or **Answer to a search request**.
* **Authorised marketing price**: the price you may communicate. Always use this one, no other.
* **Availability**: **Available**, **Reserved** or **Not available**.
* **Commercial status**: **On the market**, **With viewings**, **With an offer**, **Reserved** or **Deal closed**.

Click **View** to open the record. If you have none yet, you will see "You have no properties assigned yet.".

### Exporting your list

The **Export (CSV)** button downloads `mis-inmuebles-clarto.csv` with all your assigned properties: reference, title,
type, location, floor area, bedrooms, bathrooms, relation, authorised price, availability, commercial status and the
date of the latest update. It is semicolon-separated and opens directly in Excel or similar.

### The property record

The record has four blocks:

* **Your collaboration**: **Relation**, **Authorised marketing price**, **Availability** and **Commercial status**,
  always up to date.
* **Property details**: **Reference**, **Property**, **Type**, **Location**, **Floor area**, **Bedrooms** and
  **Bathrooms**.
* **Shared viewings**: shown when there are viewings. Each row has **Date**, **Viewing status** (**Scheduled**,
  **Done**, **Cancelled** or **Postponed**), **Commercial outcome** and **Next steps**.
* **Follow-up**: the updates CLARTO has sent you, newest first, with date and time (Spanish time). Each one has its
  title (for example **Collaboration started**, **Price change**, **Availability change**, **Viewing**, **Offer**,
  **Reservation**, **Closing** or **Other update**) and, depending on the case, the new price, the availability, the
  viewing, the **Terms to communicate** and the **Attachments**, which you download by clicking their name. If there
  are none yet, you will see "No shared updates yet.".

### Property notices

Each update reaches you **by e-mail and WhatsApp** at the same time, with the complete content, so you do not need to
open the panel to understand it. The e-mail subject is **CLARTO · (kind of update) · (reference)**. When a property is
assigned to you, you usually receive a first notice with its summary and your collaboration.

* You can **reply directly** to the e-mail or the WhatsApp message.
* The same update stays in the property record, under **Follow-up**, with its attachments.

### When a collaboration ends

If CLARTO withdraws your assignment of a property, it disappears from your panel at once and you receive no more
notices about it. If your collaboration with CLARTO is deactivated, you lose access to the panel and notices stop.

## Importing your listings

If you agree with CLARTO that we bring in your listings from your feed or website, this is what it means for you:

* **Only with your written authorisation.** Without it, nothing is read from your feed or website.
* **Source.** Your feed (Kyero XML v3, or another XML or JSON) or your website. On the website our crawler identifies
  itself as ClartoBot, respects your robots.txt and leaves several seconds between requests. It reads daily or when
  agreed.
* **They come in as drafts.** No listing is published on its own: the CLARTO team reviews it first.
* **You see them in your panel.** Each imported listing appears in **My properties** with the relation **Brought by
  the collaborator**. This entry does not send you a notice.
* **They are published as CLARTO properties, without your brand.** On clarto.es they carry CLARTO's reference. Your
  phone, e-mail, website, name or brand and your references are removed from titles and descriptions. Photos are served
  by CLARTO without their metadata, and your virtual tour is not published.
* **Watermarks.** They are only removed from photos if you expressly authorise it. The original photo is kept.
* **Synchronisation.** If you change a listing at the source, it is updated. If you remove it, the property is
  withdrawn at CLARTO (not deleted) and, if it reappears, it comes back as a draft. A property already sold or let is
  not touched.

## Requests received

When CLARTO is looking for a property for a client, it sends you a **search request** with everything being looked
for. If you have something that fits, or can get it, you propose it to us.

### How requests reach you

Each request reaches you **by e-mail and WhatsApp**, complete: title, reference (**SOL-YYYY-NNNNNN**), operation, type,
area, budget, floor area, requirements, dates and examples. A long WhatsApp arrives as several numbered messages. The
subjects you may receive are:

* **Property wanted: (title)**, the request.
* **Search updated: (title)**, with what changed at the top and the complete information.
* **Clarification about the search: (title)**, when the team needs to make something clear.
* **Reminder: (title)** and **New deadline: (title)**, about the deadline.
* **Search covered, you can stop looking: (title)**, when it has been found.

### Your personal link

Each message ends with a **personal link** to see the request and answer without opening the panel or needing a
password. It is yours alone: **do not forward it**. It works for that request and shows your earlier answers.

* Each message carries its own link, and all of them keep working while the request is open.
* Opening the link (or your e-mail or WhatsApp showing a preview of it) does not count as an answer.
* If you see **"This link is no longer available"**, use the link in the last message we sent you or reply to that
  message.

### The request list and record

**Requests received** shows the columns **Reference**, **Title**, **Operation**, **Town**, **Budget**, **Reply by** and
**Status**. Click **View** to open the record: at the top, the complete request and its **Attachments** (plans, sheets
or photos you can download); below, **Your answers**, with the date and channel of each one (**Panel**, **E-mail**,
**WhatsApp** or **Answer link**).

### Answering a request

In the record click **Answer** (or use the **Your answer** form of your personal link) and choose an option:

* **I have a property that fits**.
* **I can look for it or get it**.
* **I need clarification**: write your question in **What needs clarifying** (it is required).
* **I cannot take it on**: so we know not to wait for you.

For the first two, fill in what you can: **Description**, **Location**, **Price (€)**, **Other features**, **Links** (on
the personal link, one per line) and **Photos or documents** (up to 10 JPG, PNG, WEBP or PDF files of 10 MB each). In
**Estimated time (answer or availability)** say when you could have it. When you send it (**Send answer** on the link)
you will see **"Answer sent to the CLARTO team."**.

### Completing an earlier proposal

To add details or photos to something you already proposed, choose it in **Complete an earlier proposal (optional)**.
It is saved as a new version (v2, v3…) and the earlier one is kept. If it is a different property, leave **It is a new
proposal**.

### Answering by e-mail or WhatsApp

You can also **reply directly** to the request's e-mail or WhatsApp message. For your answer to be matched correctly,
reply to the message itself (or quote it on WhatsApp) or include the reference **SOL-…**. These answers appear under
**Your answers** as **Reply by message (not classified)**. Read receipts and automatic replies (such as "out of
office") never count as an answer.

### Deadlines and reminders

Each request has a **Reply by** date, shown in the list, the record and every message. You can answer until the end of
that day (Spanish time).

* If you have not answered yet, you receive **reminders** a few days before the deadline.
* If the deadline is extended or the request reopened, you receive **New deadline** with the new date and the complete
  request.
* An answer by message that arrives after the deadline is not accepted.

### Request statuses

The **Status** column shows:

* **Sent · no answer**: we have sent it to you and you have not answered yet.
* **Answered**: we have at least one answer from you. You can keep completing it while it is open.
* **Covered**: the property has been found. You can stop looking.
* **Closed**: the search has been closed.
* **Expired · opportunity lost**: the deadline passed without it being closed.

### If the request closes or you are withdrawn

When a request is covered, closed or expired, the **Answer** button disappears and your link shows **"This request no
longer accepts answers"**. Your answers remain visible in the panel.

If CLARTO withdraws you from a request, it disappears from your panel, you receive no more messages about it and your
link shows **"This request is no longer assigned to you"**. If you think it is a mistake, reply to the last message.

## Your data and privacy

* CLARTO uses your name, e-mail, WhatsApp number and language to give you access and send you notices. If they change,
  tell your CLARTO contact; they cannot be edited from the panel.
* The photos and documents you send with your answers are stored privately: only you and the CLARTO team see them.
* Your panel never shows data about owners, buyers, tenants or other collaborators, and they do not see yours either.

## Frequently asked questions

**I cannot see a property I expected.** Only properties with an active assignment appear. Ask your CLARTO contact.

**The invitation link does not work.** It works only once and expires. Use **Forgot password?** or ask for the
invitation to be sent again.

**Can I change the price or details of a property?** Not from the panel. Reply to the notice or write to your CLARTO
contact.

**Can I answer a request without opening the panel?** Yes: with your personal link, or by replying to the e-mail or
WhatsApp message.

**I cannot see the Answer button.** The request no longer accepts answers: it is covered, closed or past its deadline.

**I made a mistake in a proposal, can I correct it?** Send a new answer choosing it in **Complete an earlier proposal
(optional)**. It becomes the latest version.

**Can I pass the link to a colleague?** No. The link is personal; if someone else should collaborate, ask CLARTO to add
them.

**Why do my imported listings not carry my brand on clarto.es?** Because CLARTO publishes them as its own properties,
without the collaborator's contact details or brand. Your collaboration is recorded in your panel.
