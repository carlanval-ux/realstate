# User manual · CLARTO Gestión Inmobiliaria

This guide explains day-to-day work with the panel. Each property is managed **only once**, here: what you publish in the
panel is what appears on **clarto.es/inmuebles** and, once activated, on the portals.

## 1. Getting started

**Signing in.** Open https://panel.clarto.es and sign in with your e-mail address and password. If the administrator has
enabled Google, you can also click **Continue with Google** (you must first connect it once from your profile, see 7.3).

**Your first password.** When an administrator registers you, you receive an e-mail **"Your access to CLARTO Gestión
Inmobiliaria"** with the button **Create my password**. The link works only once and expires after 24 hours. If it expires,
ask the administrator to send you another.

**Forgotten your password?** On the sign-in screen click **Forgotten your password?** and you will receive a link.

**Language.** At the top right is the **ES / EN / TR** selector. The panel remembers each user's language.

**The menu.** On the left: **Sales & lettings** (Properties, Listings, Viewings, Offers…), **People & relationships**,
**Marketing & portals**, **Organisation** (users and roles) and **Platform settings** (administrators only).
At the top, **Search** finds properties, contacts and more by name or reference. The icon at the top left
collapses the menu.

## 2. Adding a property

1. Menu **Sales & lettings → Properties → New property**.
2. **Basic information** tab: **Deal type** (sale or letting) and **Property type** are required.
3. **Public texts → Spanish** tab: **Title** and **Long description** (what the client will see). See section 4.
4. **Address and legal details** tab: the **address** is required but **never published**; the website shows only
   the area. Choose **Region**, **City** and **District**: these are what the website's filters use.
5. **Price and features** tab: **price**, **bedrooms**, **bathrooms** and **Built area (m²)**.
6. **Description** tab: **Internal notes**, which only the team can see.
7. Click **Create**. The property starts as a **Draft**: it is not yet visible on the website.
8. Open it again and add the photos (section 3).

The **reference** (for example **CL-2026-00012**) is assigned by the panel. Use it when speaking to clients: it appears on the website, in
WhatsApp and in viewing requests.

## 3. Photos and videos

In the property record, **Photos and videos** tab:

* **Drag** the photos into the box or click to choose them (JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 15 MB each; on a mobile phone
  you can choose from the gallery).
* **Drag** the thumbnails to reorder them. **The first one is the cover photo** on the website.
* To remove a photo, click the **X** on its thumbnail.
* The **plans** go in their own box and are shown separately on the website's property page.
* **Videos**: upload them (MP4, WebM or MOV, up to 60 MB each) or click **Add video link** and paste a YouTube or
  Vimeo link. Drag them to reorder, like photos.
* Click **Save changes**. The website automatically creates lightweight versions of each photo.

Tip: landscape, bright photos with no people or number plates. iPhone photos in HEIC format are not accepted:
on the iPhone, go to **Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible**.

## Client and property documents

Documents are kept **on the record they belong to**, not in a general list. They are stored privately and only staff
of your company can download them.

**On the property**, **Documents** tab: land registry extract (nota simple), energy certificate, plans, certificate of
occupancy, IBI, deeds, licences, owners' association, contracts… Click **Upload document**, choose the **category**,
add a title if you like and choose the file (PDF, image, Word, Excel or OpenDocument, up to 20 MB). **Download** opens
it; the bin deletes it (with its file; this cannot be undone). Documents filed in the old «Documents and photos» list
are shown below, under **Earlier documents**.

**On the client** (owner 360 page, **Documents** tab; other contacts, **Documents** section below the form):

* **Client documents**: ID card/NIE, deeds, contracts, powers of attorney, proof of solvency, bank certificate…
  **Identity** and **solvency** documents are only shown to and downloaded by administrators and users holding the
  rental permissions **«alquileres.identidad»** or **«alquileres.solvencia»**.
* **Generated by other modules**: what the panel produced for this client (signed documents and their audit log,
  valuation reports, delivery notes, rental documents). The title opens the original and **Download** gets the file,
  with the same permissions as its own screen.
* **Linked properties**: the client's properties (owned, rented, signed or invoiced), linked to their records.

The old general **Documents and photos** list is no longer in the menu. Super administrators keep it as
**Operations & diagnostics → General archive**, for audits.

## 4. Public texts and translations (ES · EN · TR)

In the property record, **Public texts** tab, there is one tab per language. Each one has **Title**, **Short description**,
**Long description**, **SEO title** and **SEO description**.

* **Spanish** is the original. Anything missing in English or Turkish is shown in Spanish.
* The orange counter (**"2 pending"**) shows how many fields are missing in that language.
* Each language is saved with **Save changes**, independently of the others.
* Publishing in Spanish does **not** require English or Turkish.

### With artificial intelligence

* **Generate ES · EN · TR with AI**: writes the Spanish text from the property data and translates it into
  English and Turkish.
* **Translate approved Spanish to EN and TR**: when the Spanish text is already right, it only translates.
* In each language, **Write draft…** or **Translate to … with AI** redoes that language only.

The AI **never publishes**: it leaves an **AI draft (not published)** below each language's fields.

1. Read it and correct it in the draft itself. If **"Check: Figures not in the property data…"** or **"Does not mention…"** appears,
   check those points.
2. Click **Approve draft**: it becomes the official text for that language. Or **Discard draft**.

If the approved Spanish text changes, the translations show **"The Spanish text changed…: review it"**. Review the
translation and click **Mark as reviewed**.

If **"The AI quota is used up"** appears, wait a few minutes and try again: anything already generated remains
saved.

## 5. Publishing, reserving and withdrawing

Status changes are made in **Properties**, using the **⋮** menu on each row:

| Status | Visible on the website? | How it is reached |
|---|---|---|
| **Draft** | No | when the property is created |
| **Available** | Yes | **Publish** (from Draft) or **Release reservation** |
| **Under offer** | Yes, with the "Under offer" label | **Mark under offer** |
| **Sold / Let** | No | **Mark sold** / **Mark as let** |
| **Withdrawn** | No | **Withdraw** |

* The website updates immediately: when a property is withdrawn or sold, its page is no longer shown and its link displays
  «Este inmueble ya no está publicado».
* **Withdrawing is final**: a withdrawn property cannot be published again. If you only want to hide it for a while,
  mark it as **Under offer** or leave it as a **Draft** before publishing it.
* What never appears on the website: exact address, internal notes, owner and the owner's contact details.

## 6. Portals: pisos.com

In the property record, **Portals** tab:

* **Publish on pisos.com**, **Update**, **Withdraw** and **Check status**.
* You can see the **status** (Not sent, Sent (not confirmed), Accepted by the portal, Published, Rejected, Withdrawn from the portal), the **pisos.com id**, the
  **last synchronisation** and the **reason** if the portal rejects it.
* **Missing before sending** lists what needs to be completed.
* Withdrawing from the portal does **not** affect the property in the panel.

At present, real submissions are **disabled** (you will see "simulation"): they will be enabled once CLARTO has the contract and the
pisos.com keys.

## 7. Users and access (administrators)

### 7.1 Registering a person

**Organisation → Users → New user**: name, e-mail address and role. On creation, the person receives the e-mail to **create
their password**. Nobody types in another person's password.

Usual roles: **admin** (the whole panel), **staff** (day-to-day work). Fine-grained permissions are in **Organisation → Roles**.

### 7.2 Resending access

In **Users**, open the record and click **Send access link** (also in the ⋮ menu of the list). The person receives
a link to choose a new password.

### 7.3 Connecting Google

Each person, while signed in: **user icon → Profile → Connected accounts → Google**. You can only connect
a Google account whose e-mail address **matches** the one in the panel. You can then use **Continue with Google** when signing in.

## 8. Platform settings (administrators)

* **Outgoing mail**: the mailbox from which the panel sends invitations and notifications (for example info@clarto.es on
  Hostinger: smtp.hostinger.com, port 465, SSL). Use **Send test e-mail** after saving.
* **Artificial intelligence**: OpenRouter key, writing and translation models, and **Test connection**. The
  paid fallback is only used if you expressly authorise it.
* **Site settings**: name, appearance and general details.
* **WhatsApp**: link the company line by scanning the QR from its phone (WhatsApp → Linked devices), see number and status, reconnect, send a test, disconnect and edit the **reusable texts** (viewing, follow-up, signing link).

## Issues and improvements

The team's forum to report a problem with the panel or ask for an improvement or change of the tool: menu
**Processes and knowledge → Issues and improvements → New topic**. Choose the **type** (**Issue**, **Improvement** or **Change**), write a
**title** and a **description** and, if you like, attach one or more **photos or screenshots** (JPG, PNG or WebP, up to
10 files of 10 MB at most). You see them before posting and can remove any added by mistake with the **×**. Your name and
company are recorded automatically.

Everyone in your company sees every topic (never another company's). The list shows the most recently active first, with
the number of replies; you can **search** and **filter** by type, status and author. Open a topic to read the whole
conversation (replies, photos and status changes, with who and when); click a photo to see it full size. Photos are
private: only your company's team sees them.

**Reply**: anyone in the team can answer with text and photos. When someone replies to or changes the status of a topic
you opened or took part in, you get a **notice in the panel's bell** (no email or WhatsApp).

**Administrators** also change the **status** (**Received**, **In review**, **Planned**, **Implemented** or
**Discarded**) from **Reply**. Nothing is deleted: everything is kept in the history.

## Company documents (management)

Archive of the company's **own** official documents, in **folders and subfolders**. Menu **Organisation → Company
documents**. Only the **responsables** see it: users with the **admin** or **super_admin** role and the company's
**owner**. The rest of the team sees neither the menu nor the files, and access cannot be given to anyone else.

**Folders** (**Folders** button on the list): on the first visit Contratos con proveedores, Documentación fiscal,
Documentación de la empresa, Contratos de colaboración, Escrituras, Poderes and Documentos de identidad de socios are
created. You can **create** folders and subfolders («Inside» field), **rename** and move them. A folder with subfolders
or case files **cannot be deleted**: move its content first.

Each record is a **case file**: an explanation, several files and a thread of annotations.

**New case file**: title, **folder**, the person or entity it refers to (optional), **case file date**, **expiry**
(optional), **Explanation** (optional, up to 10,000 characters: what it is and why you upload it; shown at the top of
the record and changed with **Edit**) and its **files**: pick or **drag several at once** into the box (**PDF**,
**image** JPG, PNG or WebP, **Word** `.doc`/`.docx`, **Excel** `.xls`/`.xlsx` or **OpenDocument** `.odt`/`.ods`, up
to 20 MB each; **up to 20 files per case file**). Each file is kept and shown under **its original name**. Word,
Excel and OpenDocument files are **only downloaded** (never opened in the browser) and are not accepted with macros.
Stored privately.

**Edit** lists the files it already has, one per row under its name: to **remove** one, delete its row. Each row can
take an optional short **description** («Annex I», «ID card front»…), which the record shows instead of the name.
Below, **Add files** uploads more, several at once too. To move a case file to another folder, **Edit** it and change
the folder.

When creating the case file, **Does it need approval?** (**No** by default):

* **No**: the case file is **Available** to the responsables, without approval and without notices. This is the usual
  choice for information documents.
* **Yes**: choose the **rule** (see **Approval** below) and, for a number, the **approvals needed**. On saving, the case file
  is **Pending approval**, you manage it and the other responsables get the notice, just like with **Ask for
  approval** (see below).

If you are the company's only responsable, the option is disabled: there is nobody else to approve it.

**Annotations**: on the record, **Add annotation** writes a note with the date and your name; it can carry **one**
attachment (PDF, image, Word, Excel or OpenDocument, 20 MB), stored and logged like the case file's files. Any responsable can annotate.
Annotations **are not edited**: whoever wrote one can **delete** it during the first **15 minutes** (**Delete** button
next to it); after that it stays. Annotating sends no notices. The notes of older documents appear as their first
annotation.

**The list** can be **searched** (title, person, explanation, annotation text) and **filtered** by folder (subfolders included), by status or
by «expired or expiring within 60 days». **Expiry** turns **red** when expired and **orange** when it expires within 60
days; on that day the responsables get a notice (bell and e-mail).

**Statuses**: **Available** (grey), **Pending approval** (amber), **Approved** (blue), **Approved with conditions**
(indigo), **Signed** (green) and **Rejected** (red). The list shows them in their colour and can be filtered by each one.

**Approval** (for instance, collaboration agreements): when creating it, or later on the record of an **Available**
case file with **Ask for approval**, choose the **rule**:

* **Number of approvals**: you choose how many, between 1 and the number of other responsables.
* **Absolute majority**: more than half of the other responsables (with 5, 3 are needed; with 4, also 3). The form
  shows the figure, e.g. «Absolute majority: 3 of 5». It is worked out when you ask: if the number of responsables
  changes later, ask again to recalculate it.

You **manage** that approval and do not vote. The other
responsables get a **bell** notice and an **e-mail** (with a link to the record; the e-mail never carries the file) and
click **Answer**:

* **In favour**, with an optional comment;
* **In favour with conditions**: the conditions must be written (up to 2,000 characters). It counts as an approval;
* **Against**, with an optional comment.

They can change their answer while it is pending. The list and the record show the count, e.g. «2 in favour · 1 with
conditions · 1 against · 1 not voted (needed: 3)», or with a majority «… (needed: 3, absolute majority of 5)», and who
answered what. If there are conditions, the record gathers them under **Conditions**, each with its author.

When the approvals needed are reached, the manager gets a notice (bell and e-mail) saying whether any of them has
conditions. Approval covers the **whole case file** and **does not move on by itself**: **Close approval** marks it
**Approved**, **Approved with conditions** (only offered if someone voted with conditions) or **Signed** (for
«Signed», upload the signed copy).

**Rejection**: if the votes against make it impossible to reach the approvals needed, the case file becomes
**Rejected** **by itself** (for instance, with 5 voters and 3 needed, at the 3rd vote against) and the manager and
every voter are notified. To try again, **Ask for approval** opens a new round and clears the previous answers.

**The record** also shows the files with **View** and **Download** (Word, Excel and OpenDocument: **Download** only)
and the **Access log**: who viewed or downloaded each
file (annotation attachments included) and when. It cannot be changed. To **replace** a file, **Edit**, add the new one
and delete the old one's row (there are no versions); descriptions are changed there too. **Deleting** a case file
deletes its files and annotations; the access log is kept.

These case files never leave the panel: not the assistant, Processes, GHL, WhatsApp, the website or the API.

## Client communications

Every client record (owner, contact, tenant, occupant or traveller) and every mandate has:

* **Contact → Send WhatsApp / Send SMS / Send e-mail**: check the **recipient** and the **text** before sending. You
  can start from a **reusable text**, filled with the record's data; fields left between braces, like
  `{enlace_firma}`, must be completed or nothing is sent.
* **Communications**: everything sent to and received from that client, in order: WhatsApp (sent and replies), SMS
  and e-mails, including Rentals notices, signature SMS and system e-mails. Each message shows the channel, the
  direction (↗ sent, ↙ received), who sent it and its status (**Sent**, **Delivered**, **Read** or **Not sent** with the
  reason). The composer with the channel selector is at the bottom.

**Contacts → Conversations** is the inbox: clients with their last message and **unread** count on the left, the
conversation on the right. WhatsApp messages from numbers that belong to no client (or to several) wait in
**Contacts → WhatsApp to link**, where you choose the client or dismiss them.

**Purpose**: service messages (viewings, payments, contracts, incidents, signatures) can always be sent;
**commercial** ones only to clients who gave their **consent**. Each company sees only its own conversations.

Today **SMS** are simulated (they do not reach the phone) until the provider is enabled; **e-mails** go out from the
**Outgoing mail** mailbox, and e-mail replies arrive in that mailbox, not in the panel.

## 9. Frequently asked questions

**I have published it and cannot see it on the website.** Check that the status is **Available** or **Under offer** and reload
clarto.es/inmuebles. If the title is empty, the website uses the type and the area.

**How do I change the price?** Edit the property, change the price and save: the website updates immediately.

**Can I undo "Withdraw"?** No. Create the property again if it comes back on the market.

**The access e-mail has not arrived.** Check your spam folder and ask the administrator to click **Send access link**.

**What does the client see on the website?** Title, descriptions, price, floor area, bedrooms, bathrooms, photos, plans, approximate
location and the reference. To request a viewing they use the form or WhatsApp, always quoting the reference.
